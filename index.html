How we lead in CSA

Each of us leads a department. Together, we are responsible for how CSA supports scholars, families, and schools. These expectations should show up in our decisions, our relationships, and our follow-through.

The work that needs our attention

Use these priorities to prepare for our conversations and connect department work. Bring the progress, the questions, and the help you need.

Leading the GRPS Way

Principal-meeting presentations and the connections CSA needs to carry forward. Presentations open in SharePoint or Google Drive using your GRPS account.

Build confidence with one well-facilitated case.

Most current CSA Leadership Team members are new to the method. Begin with A Difficult Conversation, then Bristol City Schools, before moving to Federal Bureau of Investigation, 2001.

How to prepare for a case discussion

Read for the decision. Mark important facts, assumptions, and missing information. Write the central problem in one sentence, identify two viable options, and arrive with a recommendation.

Use the case evidence. Listen for what you missed. Understand the case before moving to a GRPS solution.

Keep the discussion on the decision.

Choose a case to prepare the 60-minute facilitator run sheet. Use the prompts to surface evidence, test options, and identify what CSA should take back to the work.
