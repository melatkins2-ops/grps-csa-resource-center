Upcoming Michigan Medicaid changes

Several changes took effect October 1, 2026, with additional changes beginning January 1, 2027. This information is shared for awareness because the changes may affect some GRPS scholars, families, and staff members enrolled in Medicaid.

Information shared by Health Services · October 2026 · Review as additional state guidance becomes available

Key changes

October 1, 2026

Immigration eligibility

Certain individuals who previously qualified for full Medicaid coverage will transition to Emergency Services Only coverage, which does not cover routine doctor visits, preventive care, ongoing treatment for chronic conditions, or most prescriptions. Most lawfully residing pregnant individuals and children under age 21 remain eligible for full coverage if they meet other requirements.

January 1, 2027

Work requirements

Some adults ages 19–64 enrolled in the Healthy Michigan Plan will need to meet work or other qualifying activity requirements, unless exempt. Requirements will be reviewed at renewals beginning March 1, 2027.

January 1, 2027

More frequent renewals

Healthy Michigan Plan members will need to renew their eligibility every six months instead of annually. Missing notices or failing to provide requested information could put coverage at risk.

January 1, 2027

Retroactive coverage

Medicaid will cover a shorter period of medical expenses incurred before someone applies for coverage, potentially leaving some individuals responsible for bills they previously would have had covered.

What this could mean for GRPS

These changes could create gaps in health coverage or additional barriers to accessing medical care, prescriptions, and behavioral health services for GRPS families and possibly staff members. There are still many unknowns.

Health Services will work with district nurses to ensure they have resources available to help families find the right information about Medicaid questions and enrollment.

Where to direct questions

School staff should not determine Medicaid eligibility or exemptions. Encourage families to review official notices, maintain current contact information, and contact MDHHS for guidance about their circumstances.
